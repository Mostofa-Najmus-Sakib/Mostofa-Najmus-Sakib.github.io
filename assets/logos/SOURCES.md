# Organization logo sources

These are original organization marks, used to identify the owner's employment
and education. No marks were generated, redrawn, recolored, or stylized. The
marks remain the property of their respective organizations.

## National Laboratory of the Rockies

- File: `nlr.svg`
- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NatLabRockies/developer.nlr.gov/main/frontend/nlr-app-template/img/nlr.svg
- Authority: the laboratory's official developer-site source in the verified
  NatLabRockies organization. This is the current NLR wordmark and mountain mark.

## Boise State University

- File: `boise-state.png` (stacked institutional wordmark)
- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEAF-BoiseState/DOA_web_viewer/master/www/BSU2.png
- Authority: Boise State's Lab for Ecohydrological Applications and Forecasting,
  whose organization links its official university lab website.
- File: `boise-state-horizontal.png`
- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cole-nielson/ibc_website/main/ibc_logos/bsulogo.png
- Provenance: a faithful university wordmark reproduction in the Boise State
  Investment Banking Club website source; secondary hosting.

## J.R. Simplot Company

- File: `simplot.png`
- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/devopsdays/devopsdays-web/main/static/img/sponsors/simplot.png
- Identification: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/devopsdays/devopsdays-web/main/data/sponsors/simplot.yml
- Provenance: the DevOpsDays event organization's Simplot sponsor artwork. Its
  metadata links `simplot.com` and `simplotcompany`; secondary hosting.
- The original transparent canvas is retained. The affiliation strip clips only
  excess transparent padding; the visible wordmark and sprout are not cropped.
